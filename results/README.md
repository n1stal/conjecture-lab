- [A counterexample to Turau’s amnesiac flooding conjecture](amnesiac-flooding/audit.md): For each k ≥ 4, a connected non-bipartite graph on 6k vertices with radius floor(5k/2) floods in at most three rounds from k sources, violating Turau’s conjectured lower bound.
- [Inverse Z-polynomials through rank four](inverse-z-rank-four/audit.md): Positive coefficients through the rank and log-concavity for matroids on finite ambient types of rank at most four. The unrestricted conjecture remains open here.
- [Property P: the finite witness {1, 2}](property-p-baseline/audit.md): The set {1, 2} has Property P and its reciprocal sum is 3/2. This is not a search certificate or an upper bound.
