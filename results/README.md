# Lean-checked results

Generated from the publication registry. Each entry links its result page and audit.

## Research results

- [A counterexample to Turau’s amnesiac flooding conjecture](amnesiac-flooding/README.md): For each k ≥ 4, a connected non-bipartite graph on 6k vertices with radius floor(5k/2) floods in at most three rounds from k sources, violating Turau’s conjectured lower bound.
- [Ordered spectral proximity forces adjacency rank at most five](spectral-rank-problem-21/audit.md): For any simple graph on 3n vertices, n ≥ 3, squared distance of its ordered adjacency spectrum to (2n, 0, …, 0, −n, −n) strictly below 2/3 forces real adjacency rank at most five. This answers Problem 21, not Conjecture 20.
- [Inverse Z-polynomials through rank four](inverse-z-rank-four/audit.md): Positive coefficients through the rank and log-concavity for matroids on finite ambient types of rank at most four. The unrestricted conjecture remains open here.
- [Strict inverse Z log-concavity for line-preserving projective restrictions](inverse-z-projective/audit.md): In every rank, the inverse Z-polynomial of a finite projective geometry restricted to points retaining at least two on every line has positive coefficients through rank and strict log-concavity at every interior degree. The unrestricted conjecture is not settled.
- [A counterexample to Van Cott–Zhang Conjecture 5.2(1)](van-cott-zhang-521/README.md): A 13-denomination system has the greedy-optimal prefix pattern prohibited for length 3r+1. Only the general prohibition in part (1) is refuted.

## Checked baselines

- [Property P: the finite witness {1, 2}](property-p-baseline/audit.md): The set {1, 2} has Property P and its reciprocal sum is 3/2. This is not a search certificate or an upper bound.
