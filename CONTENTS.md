## Precise scope

- **[A counterexample to Turau’s amnesiac flooding conjecture](results/amnesiac-flooding/).** For each k ≥ 4, a connected non-bipartite graph on 6k vertices with radius floor(5k/2) floods in at most three rounds from k sources, violating Turau’s conjectured lower bound. Does not refute Turau’s weaker proved bound in Theorem 4.
- **[Chen’s total-domination conjecture for the delta complement](results/chen-delta-complement-total-domination/).** Full Pinhe Chen Conjecture 4 for all finite undirected loopless graphs whose graph and δ-complement are isolate-free, removing the per-degree-class nonisolation condition of Chen’s Theorem 6. Sharpness is already established by Chen’s Example 3.
- **[Ordered spectral proximity forces adjacency rank at most five](results/spectral-rank-problem-21/).** For any simple graph on 3n vertices, n ≥ 3, squared distance of its ordered adjacency spectrum to (2n, 0, …, 0, −n, −n) strictly below 2/3 forces real adjacency rank at most five. Answers Problem 21, not Conjecture 20.
- **[Reflection of exceptional breakpoints for k-wise independent bits](results/beka-exceptional-breakpoints/).** Establishes Conjecture 5.11 of Berend, Ernst, Kontorovich, and Kumar over its full stated range of n and even k with 2 ≤ k ≤ n − 1. Does not address rationality of breakpoints or lexicographic indexing of bases.
- **[Counterexamples to Van Cott–Zhang Conjecture 5.2(1) and (3)](results/van-cott-zhang-521/).** The prefix pattern exists at every length at least 11. Counterexamples to the general prohibition in 5.2(1) occur at every length 13, 16, 19, …, and to the D/G classification in 5.2(3) at every length 14, 17, 20, …. A companion Palomar-registered proof establishes the length-7 ban in 5.2(1). Length 10 and part (2) at length 9 remain open.
- **[Counterexample to Cazenave’s second spectral graph conjecture](results/cazenave-second-spectral/).** Full negation of Cazenave’s Conjecture 2 (#20829) via an explicit 11-vertex connected simple graph using exact algebraic and rational certificates. No order-minimality claim; the written infinite-order extension is outside the Lean comparison target.
- **[Inverse Z-polynomials through rank four](results/inverse-z-rank-four/).** Positive coefficients through the rank and log-concavity for matroids on finite ambient types of rank at most four. The unrestricted conjecture (ranks ≥ 5) remains open here.
- **[Sharp distance packing for four prescribed terminals](results/four-terminal-packing/).** Exact four-terminal distance-packing extremum for all positive integers k and p, with unweighted and nonnegative integer-weighted objectives and explicit attaining connected graphs. Excludes Chung’s (1988) unweighted k = 1 special case from novelty; five or more terminals, real weights, and the disjoint-separator branch of Question 3 remain open.
- **[Strict inverse Z log-concavity for line-preserving projective restrictions](results/inverse-z-projective/).** In every rank, the inverse Z-polynomial of a finite projective geometry restricted to points retaining at least two on every line has positive coefficients through rank and strict log-concavity at every interior degree. Prior projective-geometry formulas and overlapping deletion methods are credited; the unrestricted conjecture is not settled.
- **[Property P: the finite witness {1, 2}](results/property-p-baseline/audit.md).** The set {1, 2} has Property P and its reciprocal sum is 3/2. This is a known baseline, not a search certificate, upper bound, or resolution of Erdős Problem 12.
