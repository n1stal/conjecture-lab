# Strict log-concavity of the ordinary inverse Z coefficients of every thagomizer

This addresses the all-n thagomizer family T_n=M(K_{1,1,n}), not inverse Kazhdan–Lusztig Q, not the companion K_{2,n} family, and not arbitrary matroids. Its rank n+1 is unbounded and T_n is nonpaving for n≥3. Based on the direct, unbounded flat classification in thagomizer-positive-formula.md and the Q_{T_k} ballot expansion from [arXiv:2510.11322](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11322), Theorem 1.2/Eq. (20). Targeted priority search did not locate this Y theorem; no historical priority assertion. This proof accompanies the finite graph-flat Lean formalization.

Let b_i=binom(n,i), extended by zero outside 0≤i≤n. To see the coefficient model, classify flats by whether the two hubs are in the same component of the spanned subgraph. In the joined case the spine is present and each included leaf has both spokes; a flat with k included leaves restricts to T_k and gives a Boolean contraction on n−k outside leaves. In the split case a leaf is isolated or assigned a single spoke to one hub. The restriction on k assigned leaves is free and the contraction simplifies to T_{n−k}. There are binom(n,k) and 2^k binom(n,k) of the two types respectively, and the absolute Möbius factors are 1 and 2^{n−k}. The latter follows by summing over proper flats and using the binomial theorem. Using the signed flat sum of [Braden–Ferroni–Matherne–Nepal, Theorem 2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01086v2), this gives Y_{T_n}=Σ_k binom(n,k)t^{n−k}Q_{T_k}+2^n t(1+t)^n. Interpret Q_{T_k} as nonnegative length-k prefixes with up, unmarked horizontal, and a degree-one down. A choice of the other n−k positions installs independent degree-one marked horizontals; removing them recovers Q uniquely. Thus Y_{T_n}=2^n t(1+t)^n+R_n(t), where R_n is the generating polynomial for length-n nonnegative Motzkin prefixes, with steps up/neutral horizontal of weight 1 and steps down/marked horizontal of weight t. Interpret each step as a pair of simultaneous bits (p_j,q_j): up (0,1), down (1,0), horizontals (0,0),(1,1). Then the height is the cumulative sum of q_j−p_j, and the t-degree is the number of ones in p.

For fixed totals i≤k of ones in p,q respectively, count the nonnegative pairs. The total is b_i b_k. For a pair that ever has height −1, swap the two prefixes up to the first such height. This produces any pair of totals i−1,k+1: the inverse swaps up to the first height +1, which must exist since the final height is k−i+2≥2. Thus the nonnegative count is b_i b_k−b_{i−1}b_{k+1}. (If i=0 or k=n the bad set and its reflected count are both empty.) Summing over k≥i and adding 2^n b_{i−1} gives
a_i=[t^i]Y_{T_n}=b_i Σ_{k=i}^n b_k + b_{i−1} Σ_{k=0}^i b_k
for 0≤i≤n+1. Every coefficient is positive including indices 0 and n+1.

At any interior index 1≤i≤n, put p=i, q=n−i+1, b=b_i, A=(Σ_{k=i}^n b_k)/b>0, H=(Σ_{k=0}^{i−1}b_k)/b≥0. Put r=p/q, v=(q−1)/(p+1), s=p(p−1)/(q(q+1)); these are b_{i−1}/b, b_{i+1}/b, b_{i−2}/b, including boundaries with zero. By tracking the tail and prefix increments, the three neighbors divided by b² are
a_{i−1}/b² = rA+r²+sH,
a_i/b² = A+r(H+1),
a_{i+1}/b² = vA+H+1.
Their normalized adjacent minor therefore equals the following exact factorization:
(a_i²−a_{i−1}a_{i+1})/b⁴
 = (p+q)[ A²/(q(p+1)) + 2pAH/(q(p+1)(q+1))
          + pA/(q²(p+1)) + p(H²+H)/(q²(q+1)) ].
Check it by expanding the quadratic in A,H; the five nonzero monomials are A², AH, A, H², H with exactly the listed coefficients. Every denominator is positive, every summand nonnegative, and the A² term is strictly positive. This proves strict LC for every interior index and no internal zeros without a cutoff in n. At n=0 the graph consists of one edge and Y=1+t (vacuous LC).
