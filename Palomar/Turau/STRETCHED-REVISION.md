For (kge4,tge3), use (k) blocks separated by interblock endpoint edges.
With (L=t-1), each block has a center and two arms of length (L), and one
extra vertex joined to the center and its two immediate arm neighbors. Seed
one center per block. Round (n=0) in Lean is physical round 1. The literal
amnesiac recurrence follows the trace in the independent Challenge.
The proof shows every vertex receives, physical sends occur exactly in rounds
1 through (t), and graph radius is (kt-lceil k/2ceil). The radius is
encoded via its full threshold equivalence, not just a lower bound.

The strict inequality (kt < kt-lceil k/2ceil+k-1) disproves the
conjecture even without optimality: the best number of rounds is at most (t).
Separately, Turau, *Analysis of Amnesiac Flooding*, arXiv:2002.10752v4,
Theorem 4(3), forces the optimum to be at least (t), since it is integral.
That published lower bound is cited, not assumed in the Challenge or added
as a formal axiom.
